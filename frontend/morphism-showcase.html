💡 Best Practices

Choose One Style

Stick to one primary morphic style throughout your design to maintain consistency and visual coherence.

Layer Purposefully

Use morphic effects to create visual hierarchy. Use depth to indicate importance and function.

Performance First

Backdrop filters can impact performance. Use them strategically and provide fallbacks for older browsers.

Accessibility

Ensure sufficient contrast. Morphic effects should enhance, not hinder readability and usability.

Interactive Feedback

Provide clear hover, focus, and active states. Users need clear feedback for interactive elements.

Test Across Browsers

Backdrop filters and advanced CSS may have limited browser support. Test thoroughly and include fallbacks.

📖 Quick Start Guide

How to Use Morphism Styles

Include the morphism.css in your HTML head:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="morphism.css">

Then apply classes to your elements:

<!-- Glassmorphism -->
<div class="glass glass-md">Content</div>
<button class="btn-glass">Click Me</button>
<input class="input-glass" type="text">

<!-- Neumorphism -->
<div class="neumorphic">Content</div>
<button class="btn-neumorphic">Click Me</button>
<input class="input-neumorphic" type="text">

<!-- Claymorphism -->
<div class="clay">Content</div>
<button class="btn-clay">Click Me</button>
